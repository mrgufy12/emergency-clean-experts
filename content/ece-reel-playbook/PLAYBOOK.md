# ECE punchy Reel cut — full playbook

## Goal
Ship one finished ready-to-post 9:16 Reel. Not a cut list.

## Brand
- ECE / Sunshine Decon — South Florida specialty restoration/remediation
- Educate; don’t scream BUY
- Optional open: “Thank you for choosing Emergency Clean Experts.”
- Never invent customer names, addresses, or results beyond brief + visible footage

## Structure
| Beat | Time | Do |
|------|------|----|
| Hook | 0–2s | Best visual or face line |
| Proof | middle | 1–3s jump cuts; drop dead air |
| Brand close | last 3–6s | Shirt/logo or thank-you caption |

## Punch tools (use ≥2)
- Slow-mo / speed-ramp on impact
- Punch-in zoom on clean line
- Before/after wipe or split
- Contrast grade (soot vs clean)

## Captions
Short beats, ~6–8 words, timed to cuts.

## Audio
Keep real job sound. Optional cleared/low bed only.

## Export
1080x1920, 30fps, H.264+AAC, ~20–35s. Deliver mp4 + caption txt.

## Tooling order
1. ffmpeg or Remotion (finished file)
2. CapCut only as optional human polish pass
