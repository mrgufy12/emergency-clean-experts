# ECE reel playbook

Shared standard for Emergency Clean Experts social Reels. Job footage, music-first cuts, captions that pop.

The quality bar is a punchy vertical edit: beat-matched picture, hotter grade, glitch and rewind transitions, and one-to-three-word captions. The repeatable version of that edit is the Remotion template, rendered on your machine.

- Full standard: [PLAYBOOK.md](./PLAYBOOK.md)
- Project: [`content/ece-remotion-reel`](../ece-remotion-reel/README.md)

Non-negotiables:

- About 20–30 seconds, 1080×1920 at 30 fps
- Music leads. Mute or duck camera audio. Cuts land on beats.
- Music must be cleared for commercial posts. The template bed is original and CC0. Do not reuse a reference edit's soundtrack or any commercial track.
- Captions: bold, white, one yellow word, heavy black outline, lower-middle, spring pop
- Remotion locally. No cloud render account, and no unofficial CapCut or music APIs.
